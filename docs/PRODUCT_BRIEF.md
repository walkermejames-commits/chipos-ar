# ChipOS AR product brief

## Device target

Primary target: XREAL Air 2 Ultra. The project must verify the currently supported XREAL SDK, Android versions, permissions, tracking interfaces, cameras, microphones, and companion-phone requirements before selecting final APIs.

## Core experiences

### Caption Lens

Continuous, adjustable live captions with optional diarization. Labels default to Speaker 1, Speaker 2, and so on. The screen must visibly show when it is listening and never save conversations without a separate choice.

### Teaching Lens

On-demand AI explanations placed sparingly in the visual field. It supports plain-English explanations, document help, object/task guidance, and one next step at a time. It must state uncertainty and not present unsafe instructions as fact.

### Sound Compass

Subtle cues for meaningful sounds. Direction is shown only when supported and sufficiently confident; otherwise the UI says that a sound was detected without pretending to know its direction.

### Spatial Awareness Assist (research)

Experimental cueing for nearby obstacles, boundaries, and low-light uncertainty. It must not be advertised as balance treatment, a medical tool, independent navigation, or a safe replacement for eyesight and physical care. It requires validation on actual hardware and may need an external depth/radar sensor.
